# Baker's Mobile Auto Detailing — Client-ready concept

Premium multi-page website concept for Baker's Mobile Auto Detailing in Concord, CA.

## Visual direction
- Real Baker's vehicle photography supplied by the client/prospect is used throughout the hero and gallery.
- Editorial automotive layout with oversized typography, dark contrast sections, warm paper backgrounds and acid-lime CTAs.
- Designed to sell the service through visual proof first, then experience, services and a simple quote flow.

## Included
- Home, Services, Gallery, About, Contact pages
- Responsive navigation and mobile menu
- Real supplied vehicle photography in `public/bakers/photos/`
- Quote/contact form UI
- Floating call/quote actions
- Privacy and Terms placeholder pages

## Before production handoff
- Confirm all public business details and phone numbers with Baker's.
- Confirm photo usage/ownership approval.
- Replace review placeholders with approved review excerpts and links.
- Connect the quote form to the client's preferred email/CRM endpoint.
- Add analytics only with the client's approval.
